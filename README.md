# Singapore PR Calculator

A calculator tool to estimate eligibility for Singapore Permanent Residence (PR) application, updated for 2026.

## Point System Documentation

This calculator uses an unofficial scoring system based on publicly known factors that ICA considers in PR applications. ICA does not publish an official point system — all applications are assessed holistically at ICA's discretion.

### Age (Maximum: 15 points)
- 21-30: 15 points
- 31-35: 12 points
- 36-40: 9 points
- 41-45: 6 points
- 46-50: 4 points
- Below 21 or above 50: 2 points

### Monthly Income (Maximum: 20 points)
- S$15,000+: 20 points
- S$10,000-S$14,999: 16 points
- S$6,000-S$9,999: 12 points
- S$4,500-S$5,999: 8 points
- S$3,000-S$4,499: 4 points
- Below S$3,000: 0 points

### Education Level (Maximum: 15 points)
- PhD / Doctorate: 15 points
- Master's Degree: 12 points
- Bachelor's Degree: 10 points
- Diploma / Professional Certification: 5 points
- Secondary / Below: 0 points

### Industry / Sector (Maximum: 10 points)
- Technology / AI / Digital: 10 points
- Healthcare / Biomedical: 10 points
- Finance / Fintech: 10 points
- Engineering: 8 points
- Green / Sustainability: 8 points
- Construction / Manufacturing: 6 points
- Education: 6 points
- Other: 3 points

### Years in Singapore (Maximum: 10 points)
- 5+ years: 10 points
- 3-4 years: 8 points
- 2 years: 5 points
- 1 year: 2 points
- Less than 1 year: 0 points

### Nationality (Maximum: 5 points)
- Malaysian: 5 points
- PRC Chinese / Indian: 4 points
- Other ASEAN: 3 points
- Other: 2 points

### Family Ties (Maximum: 15 points)
- Married to Singaporean/PR: 15 points
- Have Singaporean child: 12 points
- Have Singaporean/PR parents: 8 points
- No family ties: 0 points

### Work Pass Type (Maximum: 5 points)
- Employment Pass (EP): 5 points
- EntrePass: 4 points
- S Pass: 3 points
- Dependant's Pass / LTVP: 2 points
- Other: 1 point

### Community Involvement (Maximum: 5 points)
- Active volunteer / community member (6+ months): 5 points
- Some involvement: 3 points
- None: 0 points

### Children in Local Schools (Bonus: 5 points)
- Yes: 5 points
- No: 0 points

### Score Categories
- **80-105: Highly Likely** — Very strong profile
- **60-79: Likely** — Good prospects
- **45-59: Moderate Chance** — Consider strengthening weaker areas
- **30-44: Low Chance** — Significant improvements needed
- **Below 30: Unlikely** — May want to wait and build a stronger profile

## Key 2026 Facts
- 35,264 PRs granted in 2024 (14-year high); ~33,000-35,000 granted annually
- Total PR population stable at ~540,000 (replacement model, not expansion)
- Singapore TFR at historic low of 0.97; median citizen age 43.7
- EP minimum salary from Jan 2026: S$5,600 (S$6,200 for financial sector)
- New REP rules from Dec 2025: 180-day grace period, no reinstatement

## Disclaimer

This tool provides unofficial estimates only and should not be considered as official guidance. ICA does not use a published point system. The actual PR application process involves holistic assessment at ICA's full discretion. ICA has no affiliation with any external migration agency or calculator.
